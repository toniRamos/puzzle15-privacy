Privacy Policy – 15 Puzzle

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

15 Puzzle is a sliding number puzzle played on grids from 3x3 to 6x6, on Android and on iPhone and iPad (where it is listed as 15 Puzzle - Slide Number Game). 15 Puzzle does not collect, store, or share any personal data directly. The game does not require user registration and does not collect personal information such as name, email address, or location.

However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information automatically to provide their services: Google AdMob (and, on Android, the mediation partner described below) and Google Analytics. They handle that data under their own privacy policies and data protection terms, which protect it at least as much as this policy does, and we do not sell or share it with anyone else.

Advertising

15 Puzzle uses Google AdMob to display interstitial and rewarded ads. On Android, AdMob may serve those ads through mediation partners — currently Meta Audience Network — which are separate data controllers with their own privacy policies. On iPhone and iPad, ads are served by Google AdMob only. These services may collect certain data automatically, including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage data
  • Approximate location, derived from the IP address

This information is managed directly by the advertising provider and is subject to their respective privacy policies. You can review their advertising practices here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/
How long Google keeps this data is explained here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention

If you see an ad that is inappropriate or not suitable for your age, report it with the AdChoices or feedback icon shown on the ad, or write to the contact address below saying what you saw.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before any personalised advertising identifier is used. Your choice is stored on your device by Google's User Messaging Platform. To withdraw or change it, delete the app and install it again: the consent message is shown again on the first launch.

On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow it when iOS asks (App Tracking Transparency). If you do not allow it, ads are not personalised and the game works exactly the same, including rewarded ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time from your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Analytics

15 Puzzle uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics, such as game events and session information, that help us improve the game. This data is associated with a randomly generated identifier and does not include personal information such as name, email address, or precise location. Google Analytics deletes these events automatically when the data retention period of the game's analytics property ends. Deleting the app stops them for good: nothing is sent once it is uninstalled.

Platform Services

On iPhone and iPad, app distribution is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). On Android, the app may use Google Play Services, which may collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. For more information, please review:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Local Data

The board you are playing, your record times and record ratios per board size, solved boards and stars, daily streaks, achievements, hint and undo credits and settings are stored locally on your device, together with a diagnostic log of the current session – device model, screen size and language – that is kept on the device and used to investigate crashes.

Some of those values travel with the anonymous analytics events described above: how many boards you have finished or left, the board size and mode you played, how many moves you made against the minimum, the stars, the time and the hints of a solved board, and your daily streak. The events themselves say when a board is started, solved or left and when a hint is used, which screens you open, and which ads, rewards, rating requests and suggested games you saw or used. Nothing else leaves your device, and everything listed here is deleted when the app is uninstalled. There is no account, no cloud save and no server: the game works fully offline. If you want the anonymous analytics linked to your installation deleted, write to the contact address below and we will ask Google Analytics to remove it.

Children's Privacy

15 Puzzle is not specifically directed at children under 13 years of age. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
